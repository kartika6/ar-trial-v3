About this prototype

Place Kartika’s couch model in a room and compare its original brown, red, and blue finishes without leaving the camera view.

Size and placement

The couch uses v2’s prototype dimensions: 84 inches wide, 38 inches deep, and 34 inches high. These are illustrative specifications, not verified dimensions of a retail product. Size is fixed while you move, rotate, or recolor it.

Room scale and the floor are estimated from camera and motion tracking. A well-lit room with visible floor detail gives the engine more information to work with.

Camera access

Start camera requests your rear camera and motion sensors. Close camera ends the AR session. Returning from another app pauses the session until you choose Continue camera. Microphone access is not requested.
